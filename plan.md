# Kada Dham – Sheetla Mata Mandir website plan

## Product shape
A Hindi-first devotional website for visitors to discover Sheetla Mata Mandir at Kade/Kada Dham, Kaushambi; understand visit logistics; browse the existing puja and offering catalogue; and continue into the repository's existing account, booking and payment-link flow. Keep the current routes and API contracts. The site should make practical next steps unmistakable without inventing temple credentials, medical outcomes, reviews, or guaranteed ceremony arrangements.

## Design direction
**Design movement:** contemporary Indian devotional editorial — tactile paper and warm sandstone, balanced with the vermilion of the real temple photographs. **Core principles:** lead with authentic place imagery, make the next action easy, show useful visit and service information early, and set honest expectations. **Color philosophy:** ivory, muted rose, vermilion and small brass accents; warmth and reverence without looking like a generic ecommerce theme. **Layout paradigm:** an editorial two-column hero, short visitor-information strip, alternating image/text stories, a focused service catalogue and direct contact CTAs; avoid a centralized dashboard-like card wall. **Signature elements:** an ॐ-inspired circular wordmark, fine brass dividers, and image panels layered with restrained devotional motifs. **Interaction:** clear labels, accessible controls, sensible keyboard focus, direct call/WhatsApp/map actions, and concise booking steps. **Animation:** subtle hover elevation and short opacity/transform transitions only; honor reduced-motion preferences. **Typography:** Playfair Display for Hindi/Latin display headlines and DM Sans for navigation, body copy and controls. **Brand essence:** a practical and welcoming digital doorway to Sheetla Mata Mandir for devotees planning a visit or arranging an offering. Personality: reverent, helpful, grounded. **Brand voice:** warm Hindi-first headlines and plain-language details; examples: “माँ के दर्शन की तैयारी, सरलता से।” and “पूजा चुनें, अपनी श्रद्धा के साथ आगे बढ़ें।” **Wordmark & logo:** a small hand-drawn-style circular ॐ seal paired with a restrained temple-name lockup. **Signature brand color:** temple vermilion, derived from the supplied entrance photography.

## Implementation
The frontend remains in `client/src/kada-app.jsx` and `client/src/styles.css`. Keep current routes and forms. Replace generic stock imagery with the photographs supplied in `prathna.zip` and a small number of relevant web-sourced Kada Dham photographs in `client/public/images/`. Use local paths in both the client catalogue and backend catalogue. Record source attribution for additional visitor images. Improve navigation, homepage, service discovery, temple details, gallery, accessibility, and mobile conversion paths.

The server's current catalogue IDs and prices, OTP flow, bookings, order views and payment links remain compatible. Use the public listing's visit hours of 5:00–11:00 and 12:30–21:00 with a note to confirm on festival days; avoid implying that hours or any unverified offering represent official temple confirmation. Keep the app's existing development OTP and manual payment-confirmation limitations clear in the relevant flow.
