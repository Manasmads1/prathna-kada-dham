# Kada Dham – Sheetla Mata Mandir website plan

## Product shape
A Hindi-first devotional service website for visitors to discover Sheetla Mata Mandir at Kade/Kada Dham, Kaushambi; plan darshan; browse the existing puja and offering catalogue; and reach the temple. Preserve the current routes, data structures, local state logic, APIs, OTP flow, booking forms, and payment-link behavior exactly. Improve presentation only.

## Design direction
**Design movement:** cinematic sacred editorial — contemporary Indian devotional imagery with the restraint of a luxury pilgrimage guide. **Core principles:** put the Mata image and next visitor action first; create depth without clutter; keep service and visit details legible; preserve calm, trustworthy expectations. **Color philosophy:** layer deep garnet and plum with sindoor, marigold, rose, warm jasmine and antique brass; use cool peacock-green notes sparingly for balance and lift. **Layout paradigm:** one focused homepage hero, floating visitor-details strip, editorial image-and-copy sections, a compact offering catalogue and prominent direct-contact actions rather than a dashboard wall. **Signature elements:** the user-supplied Sheetla Mata image as a jewel-framed photo seal; fine double brass borders; glowing floral/radial halos and softly drifting light. **Interaction:** clear calls to action, mobile-first controls, focus-visible rings, gentle elevation and no sideways-scrolling filter rail. **Animation:** layered hero entrance, slow image zoom, floating inset image, orbiting light rings, staggered card reveal and polished hover feedback; never obscure controls or content, and disable nonessential movement under `prefers-reduced-motion`. **Typography system:** Playfair Display for expressive devotional headlines, DM Sans for concise wayfinding and readable body copy. **Brand essence:** an elegant, practical digital doorway to Sheetla Mata Mandir for devotees planning darshan or an offering; reverent, warm, clear. **Brand voice:** concise Hindi-first guidance with plain English support; examples: “माँ के दर्शन की तैयारी, सरलता से।” and “दर्शन, पूजा और रास्ता—एक ही जगह।” **Wordmark & logo:** pair the existing Kada Dham wordmark with the user-supplied Mata portrait cropped inside a circular, brass-edged seal; use the same image as the browser icon and social preview. **Signature brand color:** rich garnet-sindoor, balanced with marigold gold and rose light.

## Implementation
Keep existing app components, routes, forms, data objects, state management and API contracts in `client/src/kada-app.jsx` and `server/index.ts`. Apply the visual system in `client/src/styles.css`; place the supplied image in `client/public/images/kada-dham/`; update browser branding in `client/index.html`. Fix horizontal overflow at its source, including the mobile category filter rail, and add robust small-viewport grid sizing. Do not modify API, authentication, booking or backend code. Keep existing photos and attribution notes.

The server's current catalogue IDs and prices, OTP flow, bookings, order views and payment links remain unchanged. Use the public listing's visit hours of 5:00–11:00 and 12:30–21:00 with a note to confirm on festival days; avoid implying that hours or any unverified offering represent official temple confirmation.
